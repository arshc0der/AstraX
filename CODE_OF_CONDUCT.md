# Code of Conduct

AstraX adopts the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (© Contributor Covenant, licensed CC BY 4.0). This is a summary in our own words; the Covenant text is the reference for interpretation.

## Our pledge
We want participation in this community to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient and welcoming; assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Credit others' work; own and correct your mistakes.
- Keep discussion on topic; help newcomers (many are students).
- Be especially careful with physics and safety claims: be accurate, state limitations.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, spam, or promotional abuse.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope
Applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat, events) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants. Report violations privately to the maintainers (see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md); use GitHub's private reporting when available). Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.

### Enforcement ladder
1. **Correction** - private note and clarity about what was inappropriate. 2. **Warning** - with consequences for continued behaviour. 3. **Temporary ban** - from interaction for a set period. 4. **Permanent ban** - for a pattern of violations or severe harassment.
