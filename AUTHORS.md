## Acknowledgements (data, ideas and references)
- **JPL Solar System Dynamics** - planetary GM, radii and the approximate Keplerian elements (E. M. Standish tables).
- **US Standard Atmosphere 1976** - Earth density and temperature layers.
- **H. D. Curtis, *Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students*** - Lambert universal-variable method and worked example used as a regression test.
- **H. J. Allen & A. J. Eggers (1953)** - ballistic entry analysis used to validate the re-entry tool.
- **K. Sutton & R. Graves (1971)** - stagnation-point heating correlation.
- **J. S. Barrowman** - slender-body stability (centre of pressure) method.
- **The Qt Company / Riverbank (PyQt6), pyqtgraph, NumPy, Pillow, PyOpenGL communities.**
